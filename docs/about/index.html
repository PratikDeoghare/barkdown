About

This site was made by barkdown init. Replace its files with your own:

  • site.bt holds the settings.
  • posts/ holds the posts, newest first on the home page.
  • pages/ holds pages like this one, which appear in the menu.
  • static/ is copied to the site as it is.
  • templates/ may override any part of the theme.
