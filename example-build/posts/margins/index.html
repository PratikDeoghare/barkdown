Notes on margins

2 November 2026. About 1 minute to read. 4 thoughts so far, the latest on 9 November 2026, 07:45.

Thoughts on margins as they come to me while building this site's theme. They will keep arriving.

4 thoughts

9 November 2026, 07:45

On a phone there is no margin, so the notes fold away behind their numbers. Tufte CSS does this with a hidden checkbox and no script at all, which is the kind of trick worth keeping.

6 November 2026, 22:10

Code wants a margin too: the language of a listing, its name in a literate program, a button to copy it. None of these is part of the code, so none of them should sit on top of it.

4 November 2026

A footnote asks the reader to leave the line and come back. A sidenote waits beside it.1 This one, for instance. The difference sounds small and is not.

2 November 2026, 08:15

Tufte's books keep a wide margin on every page, and use it: notes, small pictures, captions. The margin is not empty space waiting for something; it is where the second voice of the book lives.
