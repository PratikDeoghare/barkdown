Welcome to barkdown

1 November 2026. About 1 minute to read.

This is the first post of a site made with barkdown. Posts are written in brashtag: plain text, with #tag{...} bags where structure is needed.

Write a paragraph, leave a blank line, write the next one. That is most of it.

Everything above the #more{} marker is the excerpt shown on the home page. The rest appears only on the post's own page.
